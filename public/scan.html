🛡️ How NCST QR Security Verification Works

  • Using Your Phone Camera App: You can also point your iPhone Camera or Android Google Lens directly at any physical NCST GadgetGuard sticker. It will automatically open the verification page.
  • Found a Gadget? Scanning a registered device allows you to immediately notify the Office of Student Affairs (Room 1109) and arrange a secure surrender without exposing the student's private contact details.
  • Official Stickers Only: All genuine NCST stickers contain a secure cryptographic token registered exclusively in the OSA database.
