About GadgetGuard

GadgetGuard is a school-wide system designed to help students, faculty, and staff register their electronic gadgets, report them as missing, and recover them through a centralized lost-and-found process managed by the Office of Student Affairs (OSA).

Register

Create your account and register your gadget.

Verify

OSA approves and generates your QR.

Recover

Report missing or claim your device.

Campus Lifecycle

How GadgetGuard Protects You

Seamless verification and custody retrieval from registration to recovery.

1

1. Register Device

Submit your laptop or phone serial number, brand, model, and photos via the portal.

2

2. OSA Verification

OSA verifies ownership and generates an official tamper-evident QR code sticker.

3

3. Native QR Scan

If misplaced, finders scan the sticker with their camera to immediately view drop-off guidance.

4

4. OSA Safe Return

Found items are secured in the OSA custody locker for claimant verification and release.

Found Item Turn-in Protocol
