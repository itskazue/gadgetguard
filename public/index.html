About GadgetGuard

GadgetGuard is the university-wide asset tracking and recovery platform managed by the Office of Student Affairs (OSA). It empowers NCST students to safeguard their laptops, smartphones, tablets, and accessories through secure cryptographic QR stickers and centralized lost-and-found custody.

1. Login

Sign in with your pre-provisioned NCST Student ID or Email.

2. Register Device

Add your device details and claim your tamper-proof QR sticker at OSA.

3. Recover

Report missing or easily retrieve your device at OSA Room 1109.
