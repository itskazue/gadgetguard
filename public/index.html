About GadgetGuard

GadgetGuard is the university-wide asset tracking and recovery platform managed by the Office of Student Affairs (OSA). It empowers NCST students to safeguard their laptops, smartphones, tablets, and accessories through secure cryptographic QR stickers and centralized lost-and-found custody.

1. Login

Sign in with your pre-provisioned NCST Student ID or Email.

2. Register Device

Add your device details and claim your tamper-proof QR sticker at OSA.

3. Recover

Report missing or easily retrieve your device at OSA Room 1109.

Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information on how gadget registration, stickers, and recovery work on campus.

How do I get an official NCST QR security sticker for my gadget? +

Log in with your NCST student account, register your gadget specifications (Brand, Model, Serial Number / IMEI), and present the device at the Office of Student Affairs (Room 1109). An OSA officer verifies the serial number and issues your tamper-evident QR security sticker.

What happens if someone scans my lost gadget's QR sticker? +

The finder will see immediate drop-off guidance to surrender the item to OSA Room 1109 or the main campus guard post. A real-time notification is generated for your account, and your private contact details remain protected and anonymous.

How do I access my student account? +

All NCST student accounts are pre-provisioned via the official Student Information System. Click the "Student Login" button in the upper right and enter your Student ID number (e.g. 2024-58694) or your active registered email.
